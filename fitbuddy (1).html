Gemini is writing your plan…

Fill in your details and press Generate. Your plan appears here.

1. You share details

Age, height, weight, goal, level, equipment and diet.

2. Prompt goes to Gemini

The app sends your profile with strict instructions to return a JSON plan.

3. Plan is shown

BMI and calories are calculated in the browser. Workouts, meals and tips come from the model.
